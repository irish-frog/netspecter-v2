## Thingiverse / Creality Notes

Published Thingiverse item:

```text
https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:7384791
```

Suggested description topics:

- NetSpecter Sophos SX/SG 125 Rev.3 rack brackets and LCD back cover.
- LCD: BigTreeTech KNOMI V2 or similar 1.28 inch 240x240 GC9A01 round TFT.
- M3 screws around 25 mm.
- PETG recommended; PLA only for test fitting.
- Print settings: 0.20 mm layer, 0.4 nozzle, 4 walls, 5 top/bottom, 40% infill, gyroid/grid/cubic, brim recommended.
- Disclaimer: not affiliated with Sophos or BigTreeTech.
